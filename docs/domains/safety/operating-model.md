# Safety: operating model

## Responsibility

The Safety context owns biometric privacy, model drift, and escalation controls. Commands include an accountable actor and correlation identifier; model outputs remain evidence, not final sporting or medical decisions.

## Safeguard

Video, biometric, and athlete development data require owner scope, consent, retention control, and human review for consequential publication or comparison.

## Acceptance signal

A feature is accepted only when tests, policy contracts, and operating evidence agree.
